How we work with you

You define the problem. We carry the risk.

  1. 01

    You bring the problem

    The process your officers struggle with, the data you cannot verify, the leak you cannot locate. You know it better than anyone — you are accountable for it.

  2. 02

    We build the instrument

    We design and build software around how your department actually works — offline-first, for real field conditions, not a head-office ideal.

  3. 03

    We deploy it — at our cost

    We put it in the field and prove it. There is no upfront charge, no tender lock-in, no capital outlay from your department. The risk of building is ours.

  4. 04

    You pay only on adoption

    Once your officers are using it, you pay a predictable monthly subscription. If it is not adopted, you pay nothing. Your budget is never spent on something that did not work.

Why there is no risk to you

Four ways your department is protected.

No budget risk

Nothing is paid upfront. No department funds are committed to software that has not yet proven itself in your own field conditions.

No adoption risk

You pay only once officers are actually using the system. If it is not adopted on the ground, the cost to you is zero.

No political risk

Our systems report the arithmetic — what the record shows. We measure; we do not accuse. The interpretation and the decision remain yours.

No lock-in

A monthly subscription, not a multi-year capital commitment. You scale it, pause it, or stop it based on whether it is working for you.

Our first instrument

Built · Ready for its first district pilot

EduAttend.AI

Verified student attendance and mid-day meal reconciliation for government schools. It gives a district officer a daily, tamper-evident view of who was actually present — and shows exactly where meal claims and attendance do not agree.

It is built and ready. It has not yet run in a school — we are looking for the first district willing to pilot it, at no cost. It is the proof of how we work: you would be the first officer to put it to the test, and you would risk nothing to do so.

How we work

We assist. We do not adjudicate.

The instrument reports what the record shows. The interpretation, and the decision, belong to the officer. We are the lens, never the judge.

We report the arithmetic, not a verdict.

A claim of thirty-eight, a verified count of thirty-five, a difference of three. We present what is measured and let it speak. Drawing the conclusion is your prerogative.

We hold ourselves to the same standard.

If a pilot fails to meet the benchmark agreed before it began, we will be the first to say so. An instrument that flatters its maker is of use to no one.
