How we work

We assist. We do not adjudicate.

The instrument reports what the record shows. The interpretation, and the decision, belong to the officer. We are the lens, never the judge.

We report the arithmetic, not a verdict.

A claim of thirty-eight, a verified count of thirty-five, a difference of three. We present what is measured and let it speak. Drawing the conclusion is your prerogative.

We hold ourselves to the same standard.

If a pilot fails to meet the benchmark agreed before it began, we will be the first to say so. An instrument that flatters its maker is of use to no one.
