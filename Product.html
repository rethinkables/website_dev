EduAttend.AI

Verified student attendance and mid-day meal reconciliation for government schools. It records not what a school types, but what a camera saw — on the device, offline, with the teacher's judgment always final.

A note in fairness: EduAttend is built and ready, but it has not yet run in a school. We are seeking the first district willing to pilot it — at no cost. If that is you, you would be the first officer to put it to the test, and you would risk nothing to do so.

What it solves

A reported number is not a verified number.

Attendance reaches state portals every day. Meal claims are filed and paid. But nothing establishes whether the figures match the children who were actually in the room — so the distance between what is claimed and what is real stays invisible.

At the mid-day meal rate of ₹8.17 per child, sanctioned by the CCEA, an unverifiable attendance figure means an unverifiable nutrition claim. EduAttend closes that gap — not by accusing anyone, but by making the ground truth visible to the officer who is accountable for it.

How it works

Four steps. Three minutes. Fully offline.

  1. 01

    Capture

    The teacher scans a code at the classroom. The rear camera sweeps the room and matches faces on the device itself. No internet is required.

  2. 02

    Seal

    Each record is fingerprinted on the device the moment it is taken. It cannot be edited or backdated afterward. Anyone unmatched starts absent; the teacher confirms with a tap.

  3. 03

    Sync

    When a network returns, records upload on their own to the district dashboard — nothing is lost, nothing is delayed.

  4. 04

    See

    The officer receives a clear daily view of who was present, and of exactly where meal claims and verified attendance do not agree.

What makes it different

Designed for the field, and for trust.

Offline-first

Works fully without internet and syncs when a network appears. Built for the schools where earlier systems failed, not for the head office.

Tamper-evident

Every record is written once, hash-chained, and cannot be altered or backdated. The number the officer sees is the number that was taken.

On-device matching

Faces are matched on the phone. Nothing leaves it but a roll number and present or absent — no images, no biometric data in transit.

Consent-first

No child's face is enrolled without written parental consent, in compliance with the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023.

Default absent

Presence must be earned by a scan or an explicit tap. Nothing is marked present by doing nothing — the design that closes the fraud a paper register invites.

Teacher's judgment is final

A support, not a monitor. There is no punitive mechanism. Honest work simply gains a record that can be trusted.
